## 0.8.5

### Added

- Cape editor: rotate the uploaded image. The slider snaps to quarter turns so exact angles are reachable, and a 90° button steps a quarter turn at a time
- Cape editor: choosing a solid cape colour now offers preset swatches, per-channel sliders, and hex entry

### Changed

- Cape editor: image scale now reaches 8x, on a slider that keeps fine control around 1x instead of spending its travel at the top end
- Solid cape colours use the launcher's own colour picker rather than the system one, which included an eyedropper button that never opened anything
